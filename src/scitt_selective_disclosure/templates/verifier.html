Verifier

Verification is independent of the researcher and of MSRC. Import a bundle, the MSRC trusted root and the transparency service trust material fetched separately from /.well-known/scitt-keys. Every check is reported on its own, and the overall result passes only if all of them pass.

Two engines run. The C++ selective-disclosure tool checks the MSRC certificate chain, the issuer signature, the disclosures and the statement binding. The SCITT receipt is checked only by the official upstream pyscitt verifier, which is given the exact registered and transparent statement bytes the bundle carries.

Fetch this independently, for example curl -k https://127.0.0.1:8000/.well-known/scitt-keys -o scitt-keys.cbor. It is stored in its own directory and handed to the official verifier as its trust store.
